About DefencePost

INDIA'S INDEPENDENT DEFENCE JOURNALISM PLATFORM

"Our mission is to provide accurate, independent, and in-depth defence journalism — covering military operations, strategic policy, geopolitics, and technology — from India and around the world."

Who We Are

DefencePost.in is an independent Indian digital media platform dedicated to defence and strategic affairs journalism. We were founded with a singular purpose: to fill the gap in credible, accessible, and analytical defence reporting in India.

At DefencePost.in, we believe that an informed citizenry is essential to a strong democracy. Defence and national security affect every Indian, and yet quality reporting on these subjects has historically been limited to specialised audiences. We change that by bringing expert analysis and breaking news to a wider readership — in clear, compelling language.

Our Values

Independence

We are editorially independent. Our reporting is not influenced by government, political parties, or corporate interests.

Accuracy

We verify information before publishing. We correct errors promptly and transparently when they occur.

Depth

We go beyond headlines to provide context, analysis, and expert perspective on complex defence matters.

Our Editorial Standards

DefencePost.in adheres to the highest standards of journalistic integrity. All our articles are written and reviewed for factual accuracy. We rely on open-source information, official statements, expert analysis, and established defence publications.

We do not publish classified information or content that could compromise national security. All opinions expressed in analysis pieces are those of the author and do not represent official positions of any government or organisation.

Contact & Contributions

We welcome contributions from defence analysts, veterans, researchers, and journalists. If you have expertise in defence and strategic affairs and would like to contribute to DefencePost.in, please reach out to us.
